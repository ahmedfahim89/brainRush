## Fact-check log (2+ sources each, web search 2026-11-16)

Sources in the search results were a mix of encyclopaedias, official pages and news outlets. Where only wiki mirrors or low-quality pages agreed, that is noted.

The question avoids award counts and the Guinness record, which sources disagree on.
- **1116-12 Justin Bieber:** Britannica and several biographies agree that Scooter Braun found him through YouTube videos; "Baby" (2010) became the most viewed YouTube video for a time. Sources disagree on the discovery year (2007 or 2008), so the question gives no year.
- **1116-13 Robert Downey Jr.:** ABC News affiliates, The Star (Malaysia), Yahoo and others confirm the first Oscar, Best Supporting Actor, for "Oppenheimer" at the 2024 ceremony, as Lewis Strauss. "Iron Man" (2008) with Tony Stark is standard knowledge, confirmed in the same coverage as his best-known role.
- **1116-14 Angela Merkel:** The official Bundeskanzler site and Britannica confirm election on 22 November 2005 as the first woman Chancellor and the end of her term in December 2021; East German upbringing and a physics doctorate (Leipzig, 1978) confirmed by EBSCO and Britannica-based pages. Born in Hamburg, taken to the East as a baby.
- **1116-15 Toothless:** Wikipedia and DreamWorks wiki pages confirm the March 2010 release, Hiccup, the Night Fury and the name Toothless. (Fan wiki plus Wikipedia; a second non-fan source such as the film's official site is advisable.)
- **1116-16 Dory:** Wikipedia (Finding Dory) and Live Science confirm Dory is a regal blue tang (Paracanthurus hepatus), and the 2016 Pixar release.
- **1116-17 Cronut:** Wikipedia, Tasting Table, The Takeout and NPR confirm Dominique Ansel, New York (SoHo), May 2013, croissant-doughnut hybrid and the queues. Launch date given as 9 or 10 May, so the question says only 2013.
- **1116-18 Microsoft:** Microsoft's 2019 and January 2023 announcements as reported by TechRepublic, Fortune and HPCwire confirm 1 billion dollars in 2019 and a multiyear, multibillion-dollar commitment in January 2023. The 10 billion figure was only reported (Bloomberg), so it is not in the question.
- **1116-19 La La Land:** ABC News affiliates and NME confirm Warren Beatty and Faye Dunaway announced "La La Land" in error at the 2017 ceremony and "Moonlight" won.
