Term: Director General from 1 December 1997; IAEA and Carnegie Endowment confirm the term ended 30 November 2009 with Yukiya Amano taking over on 1 December 2009.
- **1116-24 Masha and the Bear:** Wikipedia (Animaccord, created by Oleg Kuzovkov, first aired 7 January 2009; the Bear is a retired circus performer), The Jakarta Post (2016 feature), Animation Magazine and Animaccord's own news pages (most-watched cartoon on YouTube). - **1116-27 Hazelnuts:** nutella.com (Ferrero: hazelnuts 13%, cocoa, milk; "hazelnut spread with cocoa") and Wikipedia (Nutella, cocoa and hazelnut spread by Ferrero). The question does not claim hazelnuts are the main ingredient (sugar and palm oil are).

- **1116-29 Stargate:** AP via local affiliates (News4Jax, ClickOnDetroit, 21 January 2025), TechRepublic, eWeek, Yahoo News: OpenAI, SoftBank and Oracle announced the Stargate venture at the White House, an initial 100 billion dollars and up to 500 billion over four years.
