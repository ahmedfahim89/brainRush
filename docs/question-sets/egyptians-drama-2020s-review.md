## How the set was built

- **Release window.** Every work, and every season used, was first shown between 2020 and 2026. The question names the year (and the season where it matters, e.g. "season 6 of El Kebir Awi (Ramadan 2022)", "El Ekhtiyar 2 (Ramadan 2021)").
- **Spread.** 16 works: 12 series and 4 films (El Sett, Kira & El Gin, Waahed Tani, El Hareefa). Each value has at least 2 different works. Three works appear twice, at values far apart: El Leaaba (100 and 600), Kira & El Gin (200 and 600), El Ekhtiyar (300) and El Ekhtiyar 2 (400). In each pair, neither question gives away the other's answer: the El Gin first-name question does not name Ahmed Ezz, and the Basiouny question does not name Mazo.
- **Question types.** 7 "which actor plays character Z", 6 "what is the name or nickname of the character played by X", 5 story or character facts (El Sett, Ashghal Shaqqa, Taht El Wesaya, Waahed Tani, El Fetewa family name).
- **Difficulty.** 100 = lead characters of the biggest hits (El Leaaba, El Kebir Awi 6, El Sett). 200 = big hits, one step less obvious (Safsaf, Paranormal, Kira & El Gin). 300 = a supporting lead or a story detail from a big Ramadan show. 400 = the classic mix-up (Karim Abdel Aziz vs Ahmed Mekky in El Ekhtiyar 2) and less-remembered facts. 500-600 = real names, family names and supporting cast.
- **Duplicates and rejected items.** Checked against all 81 Egyptians rows and the whole bank's answers. Amir Karara (El Mansi, Egyptians 300) and El Hashashin (Egyptians 300) are existing answers, so they are never named or used as options. No answer repeats an answer in the bank: "Salah" (Neamet El Afokato) and "A robot" (the 2021 film "Mousa") were dropped for clashing with Mohamed Salah / Salah Zulfikar and "Humanoid robots" (Sports 200). Nothing on the owner's rejected list (`docs/question-sets/rejected-answers.md`, Egyptians section: Ahmed Ramzy, El Bostagy, the bull proverb, Doaa El Karawan, Bab El Hadid, El Azima, El Mummia, Rihani / Kishkish Bey, Sayedati El Gameela) is used. Note: that file is not on `main` yet. It was read from the `../BrainRush-auto-question-topup` worktree.
- **Names shared with other questions (same practice as the existing Egyptians set).** Some answers here appear as plain names in the text of existing Egyptians questions, and the other way round, without giving away the link that is asked: Karim Abdel Aziz (named in the El Feel El Azraq questions), Ahmed Ezz (named in the Welad Rizk question), Mona Zaki (Egyptians 200 answer, named here in the El Sett, Taht El Wesaya and Leabet Newton questions) and the word "Ramadan" in "Ramadan series" (Egyptians 500 answer "Ramadan", the father in El Eyal Kebret). The existing El Ekhtiyar 300 question already says "2020 Ramadan series". If you want a stricter rule, the 3 Mona Zaki mentions are the ones to rephrase.

## Dropped or reworded during fact-checking

- **El Leaaba Season 1 date.** The brief said Season 1 was 2018. Arabic Wikipedia gives a premiere of 24 April 2020 (MBC Masr and Shahid). elCinema lists the work as 2020 (15 January 2020 on Shahid). So every season (1-5) is within 2020-2026. The questions just say "(The Game, 2020)".
- **"Basiouny El Malt" (owner's example).** No source gives the surname "El Malt". elCinema lists the role as "مستر بسيوني" and Arabic Wikipedia as "بسيوني زوج شويكار", both played by Samy Maghawry. The question uses "Mr. Basiouny, the husband of Shwikar" and drops "El Malt". Please confirm if you remember a surname from a later season.
- **Hesham Maged's character.** One Al-Ahram article on Season 5 swaps the leads (Waseem = Hesham Maged). elCinema, Arabic Wikipedia, El Watan, Hia and Sayidaty all give Hesham Maged = Mazo and Shiko = Waseem El Sereity, so Al-Ahram was treated as an error.
- **El Ekhtiyar 2 roles reversed in my first draft.** I first had Karim Abdel Aziz as Youssef El Rifai and Ahmed Mekky as Zakaria Younes. elCinema, Arabic Wikipedia and Cairo24 give Karim Abdel Aziz = Zakaria Younes and Ahmed Mekky = Youssef Refaie. Two early articles (Cairo24, Sout Al-Omma) guessed before the premiere that Youssef turns terrorist. That contradicts the show, so the question avoids any plot claim about Youssef.
- **Dates corrected.** Taht El Wesaya aired in Ramadan 2023 (from 7 April 2023), not 2022. Leabet Newton aired in Ramadan 2021. El Fetewa aired in Ramadan 2020 (24 April 2020).
- **Taht El Wesaya.** I first planned to ask Mona Zaki's character name, but elCinema lists her as "حنان عبدالحميد السيد - ليلى" (the boat is renamed "Laila"). That could make the name ambiguous, so the question asks for her trade instead.
- **Waahed Tani.** elCinema lists Ahmed Helmy as "مصطفى / اكس": Mustafa and his wild other self "X". A name question would be ambiguous, so it asks where Mustafa works.
- **Gaafar El Omda wives.** The order of the 4 wives differs between sources (Khabrabyad swaps the 2nd and 3rd, and an early El Watan article gave wrong names), so no wife question was used. Hala Sedki's role (El Hagga Wasifa, nicknamed Safsaf) is consistent everywhere.
- **Neamet El Afokato.** Reworded from "what is the husband's name" (Salah) to "which actor plays Salah" (Ahmed Zaher), because "Salah" overlaps existing answers (Mohamed Salah, Sports 100. Salah Zulfikar, Egyptians 600).
- **"Mousa" (2021 film).** Dropped. "What is Mousa?" ("A robot") overlaps the Sports 200 answer "Humanoid robots". "Which actor plays Yehia?" (Karim Mahmoud Abdel Aziz) is too easy to confuse with the Karim Abdel Aziz answer in this set.
- **"Bolbol Hayran".** Dropped: it is a 2010 film, outside the window.
- **"Siko Siko" (2025).** Not used: the plot is about selling an inherited drug stash, which is not a good fit for a party game.
- **"Kamel El Adad" (2023).** Not used: the child count (4 + 3 = 7 in season 1) came from only one compiled source, and it changes in later seasons.
- **"Fi Kol Esbou' Youm Gom'a" (2020).** Not used: Menna Shalaby's character has two names (Nour and Laila), and the supporting cast's roles could not be confirmed.
- **El Sett release.** World premiere at the Marrakech festival (late November 2025). Egyptian cinema release 10 December 2025. Inside the window.
