- **OWNER FLAG, freedom of panorama:** Commons lists Qatar as "Freedom of panorama = No" (Commons:Copyright rules by territory/Qatar). The stadium (designed by Dar Al-Handasah, finished 2021) is a protected work, so showing a photo of it may need the rights holder's permission in Qatar and in countries that apply Qatari rules. The bank already has the same kind of risk for UAE, Egypt and Kuwait buildings (landmarks-review.md, decision 2), which the owner kept. The owner accepts this only with a clearly licensed photo; the photo's licence here is clear.
- **Download:** on 2026-11-12, the full-size file from upload.wikimedia.org (English Wikipedia's media store) into a new, empty temp directory, with a named User-Agent. 1430 x 831 px; its sha1 matches the file page. The original carried EXIF data (iPhone 8, GPS).
- **Re-encoding:** with PHP GD: cropped to x 0-1400, y 150-715 (removes the cabs of two construction machines, a truck's lower half and a worker at the right edge), resized to 1280 x 517, JPEG quality 82, 61,121 bytes. Pixels only, so the EXIF/GPS data is gone.
- **Visual check:** the final file was viewed. It shows the whole white tent with black and red stripes and the red entrance arch against an evening sky. The top of a truck, an excavator arm and lamp posts are in front. Tiny dots on the roof edge may be workers, too small to see as people. Photo from January 2020, when the outside was nearly finished; the tent looks the same today.
