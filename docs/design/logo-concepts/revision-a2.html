Concept A, revision 2

Same idea, same palette: a brain leaning forward with speed streaks, gold on navy. The drawing is redone to fix the critic's findings. Left column is the current A, right column is A2.

What changed

  • No face. The five closed, same-width fold strokes are gone. A2 has three tapered sulci that each enter from the outline: the central sulcus from the top, a short parieto-occipital cut at the back, and the lateral fissure rising from the lower front. No closed hooks, no two marks side by side, no line along the bottom. Checked at 32 px and upside down below.
  • Outline. Five bumps of different sizes instead of four identical radius-46 arcs: largest at the front (radius 58), crown 44, parietal 38, and a flatter back (radius 76). The lower back bulge is now a small cerebellum tucked under a notch, with a short square-ended brain stem, so it no longer reads as a jaw.
  • Streaks sit inside the same skewX(-12) as the brain and use the same gold gradient (one user-space gradient for both). The unused horizontal gradient is removed.
  • Header mark (concept-a2-mark.svg): no tile, no border, cropped to the art. At 2.2rem (37.4 px) tall the gold art is 36.6 px tall, against 17 px for the brain inside the old boxed icon (measured).
  • Favicon (concept-a2-favicon.svg): its own 32-unit drawing, navy tile with no border, one fold, two streaks. At 16 px there is at least one fully navy pixel column between each streak and the brain (measured on the raster).
  • Maskable: all gold within 196.5 px of the centre (safe circle 204.8 px), 0 gold pixels outside. The art is larger than in A, which reached 189.1 px.
  • og-image: no gold frame on the image edge; frameless mark, tagline "Team trivia, buzzer speed".
  • Wordmark: no outer pill; frameless mark plus "Mokh Rush", and an Arabic lockup with مخ رش in the system font.
  • Open point: the mark moves to the right, against Arabic reading direction. It is not mirrored in the RTL lockup; the owner can decide whether an RTL variant is wanted.
  • Contrast (computed with the WCAG formula): gold #f5c518 on #0b1026 is 11.54:1, light gold #ffe27a 14.7:1. Gold on a light tab #f1f3f4 is only 1.46:1, so the favicon keeps its navy tile (navy on #f1f3f4 is 16.9:1). On a dark tab (#35363a) the navy tile is only 1.56:1, but the gold inside it is 7.4:1 against that tab, so the mark still stands out.
