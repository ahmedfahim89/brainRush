Mokh Rush is run by Ahmed Fahim. The quickest way to reach us is by email.

Email

ahmedfahim89@gmail.com

We read every message and reply as soon as we can. Please write in English or Arabic.

What you can write about

  • A wrong or outdated answer. Tell us the category, the points and the question text, and what you think the right answer is. A link to a source helps.
  • A photo or photo credit. If a picture is wrong, or you own a photo and its credit is missing or incorrect, tell us the question and the photo.
  • Ideas. Suggest a new category, a question, or a feature you would like in the game.
  • Your data. Ask us to remove your name from the streak leaderboard or from our game log. See the Privacy Policy for what we keep.
  • Anything else, such as a problem with the site or a question about the Terms of Service.

Before you write

Questions about the rules, scoring or helpers are probably answered in How to play. To learn more about the game and who makes it, see About Mokh Rush.
