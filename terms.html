Your data

On our server

When a game starts, we save the date and time, the player or team names (for a Streak Challenge, the challenger name you enter), and the chosen categories in a daily log file. Only the site owner can read it. We use it to see how the game is played. We never sell or share it. To have a name removed, email us.

When a Streak Challenge run ends with a streak of 1 or more, we save the challenger name you entered, the streak and the date and time of the run. The public streak leaderboard shows everyone the top 10, one row per person with their best run, name and date, so use a name you're happy to show. The site owner can remove a run at any time; to have yours removed, email us.

In your browser

The running game (or Streak Challenge run) and the list of questions you've played are saved in your browser (local storage), so a refresh doesn't lose your game and new games pick questions you haven't seen. To choose those questions, the game sends the numbers of the played questions to our server, but nothing that identifies you. Forget played questions on the setup screen, or clearing this site's data in your browser, removes the list.
