Who runs Mokh Rush

Mokh Rush is a personal project run by Ahmed Fahim ("we" on this page). There are no user accounts, and we don't run analytics of our own.

On our server

When a game starts, we save the date and time, the player or team names (for a Streak Challenge, the challenger name you enter), and the chosen categories in a daily log file. Only the site owner can read it. We use it to see how the game is played. We never sell or share it. To have a name removed, email us.

When a Streak Challenge run ends with a streak of 1 or more, we save the challenger name you entered, the streak and the date and time of the run. The public streak leaderboard shows everyone the top 10, one row per person with their best run, name and date, so use a name you're happy to show. The site owner can remove a run at any time; to have yours removed, email us.

Question reports

When you use Report on a question, we save the number of the question, the reason you picked, your note if you wrote one (up to 200 characters) and the date and time. We don't save your name, your IP address, a device id or anything else that identifies you, so please don't put personal details in the note. Only the site owner reads reports, to fix questions. The owner marks each report as resolved or dismissed; it is deleted together with its question.

In your browser

The running game (or Streak Challenge run) and the list of questions you've played are saved in your browser (local storage), so a refresh doesn't lose your game and new games pick questions you haven't seen. To choose those questions, the game sends the numbers of the played questions to our server, but nothing that identifies you. Forget played questions on the setup screen, or clearing this site's data in your browser, removes the list.

The bets of Golden Goal are saved with the running game in your browser and are never sent to us. Your Read questions automatically choice and your interface language (EN / AR) are also saved only in your browser. Reading a question aloud uses the voices of your device and browser; we receive nothing. Most voices work on the device itself, but some browsers offer online voices, which send the question text to the browser maker's speech service. Mokh Rush uses a voice on your device whenever there is one.

Your Daily Challenge run and today's result are also kept only in your browser, so the game can show your result again the same day. We don't save Daily Challenge results on our server: it only stores which questions belong to each day. Share passes the result text to your phone's share menu or copies it to your clipboard; nothing is sent to us.

Ads and cookies

Mokh Rush may show ads from Google AdSense on the Streak leaderboard screen, never during a game. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show these ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud. Ads may be personalised based on your earlier visits to this and other websites.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google asks for your consent before using cookies for ads. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy link in that message.
  • To learn how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • To turn off personalised ads from Google, use Google Ads Settings. For other ad companies, see aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.eu.
  • An ad blocker hides the ads and doesn't affect the game.

Mokh Rush itself doesn't set any tracking or analytics cookies.

Android app

The Mokh Rush app for Android opens this same website full screen in Chrome on your phone. It saves the same data as the website, as described on this page, and adds nothing of its own: no account and no tracking. The app shows no ads, so the Ads and cookies section above applies to the website only. Installing the app from Google Play is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Claude and ChatGPT connector

The Mokh Rush Trivia plugin and connector let you play Mokh Rush questions in Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant, and the connector also works in OpenAI's ChatGPT. Neither one has an account or a sign-in, and neither one saves anything about you on our server.

  • In Claude Code, the plugin asks our server for questions and sends the numbers of the questions you've already seen, but nothing that identifies you. Your score, that list and your on/off setting are saved by Claude Code on your own computer.
  • In Claude's chat and Cowork, the trivia card comes from our connector. When the card opens or needs more questions, Claude's app asks our server for them and sends the numbers of the questions the card already showed. The connector receives nothing from your conversation, and we don't save these requests. The card loads its script and pictures from this website, so your browser connects to our server as it does when you visit. Your score lives only in the card while it's open.
  • In ChatGPT, the same connector shows the same card and works the same way: ChatGPT asks our server for questions with the numbers of the questions the card already showed, and nothing from your conversation.
  • What you write to Claude, and how Claude handles it, is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy. For ChatGPT, see OpenAI's privacy policy.

The standard hosting logs described below apply to these requests too.
